### 🛠️ What We're Building

Most people with PRRT2 spend years — sometimes decades — unable to describe what is happening inside their own nervous system. Symptoms are episodic, neurological, and invisible to everyone else. Misdiagnosis is the norm, not the exception.

The knowledge base you're reading is the foundation. Beyond it, the PRRT2 Gene Alliance is building technology to give every PRRT2 patient a clinical voice from day one.

{% hint style="success" %}
**🧭 PRRT2.org Perspective**

_The following reflects the organizational goals of the PRRT2 Gene Alliance. These tools are in active development._
{% endhint %}

**Visual disease explainer**\
An animated, medically grounded illustration of what PRRT2 dysfunction looks like at the cellular and neurological level — in motion, in plain language. Designed so a patient can show a new clinician exactly what is happening inside their nervous system during a paroxysmal episode or dystonic event.

**PRRT2-specific symptom tracker**\
A structured episode and symptom logging tool built around the real PRRT2 constellation — not a generic symptom diary. Tracks triggers, episode type, duration, motor and sensory features, and recovery arc. Exports a clinical summary formatted for a neurology appointment.

**Diagnostic navigator**\
An intake tool that maps a patient's symptom history against known PRRT2 presentations and generates language a patient can bring directly to their physician — designed to shorten the diagnostic odyssey that affects the overwhelming majority of PRRT2 patients.

These are the organizational goals of the PRRT2 Gene Alliance. [Read the full mission →](about.md#our-goals-as-an-organization)
