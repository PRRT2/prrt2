# What We're Building

The PRRT2 Foundation, Inc. is an early-stage nonprofit. This page describes what we are actively working toward — not a completion list, but a clear account of where we are heading and why.

***

## The knowledge platform

PRRT2.org is the Foundation's first major project, and it is live and growing. The immediate development priorities are:

* **Completing clinical coverage.** Several pages are in draft. The full medications section, the research pipeline overview, and the clinical trials tracker are all being expanded.
* **Expanding the diagram library.** The basal ganglia mechanism and patient profile diagrams are published. Additional visual explainers — ion channel behavior, inheritance patterns, symptom constellation mapping — are in development.
* **Improving search and discoverability.** Making PRRT2.org visible to people searching for their symptoms years before receiving a diagnosis is one of the highest-leverage goals at this stage. We are actively working toward this.

***

## The patient registry

No structured registry of PRRT2 carriers currently exists. The scientific literature reports prevalence estimates, but the actual carrier population is scattered across geographies, specialists, and diagnostic categories — with no mechanism to aggregate their experiences into a dataset that could inform research.

We are building one. The goal is a consent-based, patient-powered registry where carriers contribute symptom history, diagnostic journeys, genetic variant data, and treatment responses. We are in the design and partnership-development phase.

***

## Research and partnership development

Accelerating PRRT2 research requires connecting the carrier community to institutions and organizations that can move science forward. We are actively working to establish formal research and advocacy partnerships. More will be announced as those relationships develop.

***

## Clinical voice tools

Most PRRT2 carriers enter the medical system without language for what is happening to them. The diagnostic odyssey — years of misattribution before anyone tests for PRRT2 — is nearly universal.

We are building tools to change that. Three are in active development and described on the [homepage](README.md):

* **A visual disease explainer** — showing what PRRT2 dysfunction looks like at the cellular and neurological level
* **A PRRT2-specific symptom tracker** — designed around the real episode constellation, exportable as a clinical summary
* **A diagnostic navigator** — maps symptom history to known PRRT2 presentations and generates language to bring directly to a physician

***

## Foundation infrastructure

The PRRT2 Foundation, Inc. is a Florida nonprofit corporation. We have filed Articles of Incorporation and are completing our IRS 501(c)(3) determination process. Nonprofit status is a prerequisite for the full range of technology, grant, and partnership access the Foundation needs to operate at scale.

The Foundation is patient-led. It is built by a carrier, for carriers, with a board that understands this condition from the inside.
