# What We're Building

The PRRT2 Foundation is in its early stages. This page gives an honest picture of where we are and the direction we are heading.

***

## Where we are now

PRRT2.org is live and actively expanding. The knowledge base is our first and current priority — completing clinical coverage, improving the diagram library, and making the site as useful as possible for patients, families, and clinicians encountering PRRT2 for the first time.

The Foundation is a Florida nonprofit corporation recognized as a 501(c)(3) public charity by the IRS, effective June 9, 2026. EIN: 42-3128330.

***

## Where we are going

Beyond the knowledge base, the Foundation's goals include a patient-powered registry to aggregate the carrier experience across geographies and diagnoses, formal research and advocacy partnerships to accelerate scientific progress, and technology tools designed to give carriers a stronger clinical voice.

These are in early development. We will update this page as the work matures.

***
