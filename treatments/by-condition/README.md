# Treatment by Condition

The [Medications](../medications/README.md) pages describe the drugs themselves. These pages take the other angle: for each PRRT2-related condition, **what the overall treatment approach actually looks like** — which options come first, what to try when, and what role lifestyle plays.

The two angles are meant to be read together. A medication page tells you what carbamazepine is; the [Treatment for PKD](pkd.md) page tells you where it fits in managing PKD.

> As everywhere in this section: informational, not medical advice. Treatment belongs to you and your specialist.

## In this subsection

* [Treatment for PKD](pkd.md) — the most treatable PRRT2 condition
* [Treatment for BFIS](bfis.md) — infantile seizures, often self-limiting
* [Treatment for PED](ped.md) — where the underlying gene shapes the approach
* [Treatment for Dystonia](dystonia.md) — paroxysmal versus focal approaches
* [Treatment for Dysphonia](dysphonia.md) — managing the voice disorder on its own terms
