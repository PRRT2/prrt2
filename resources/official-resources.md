# Official Resources

This page collects the primary, authoritative sources on the PRRT2 gene in one place — the core institutional references from MedlinePlus, GeneReviews, OMIM, and related bodies. These are the stable, consensus-level resources: the foundational science that the broader medical and research community has established, and what you would share with a healthcare provider or use as a starting point for your own research.

**A note on how citations work across this site:**

Throughout PRRT2.org, individual pages also cite the specific research literature behind their content — published studies, case reports, and reviews that support particular claims, especially in areas where the science is still developing or where this site presents emerging evidence that goes beyond the consensus baseline. Those citations appear inline on each page and are separate from the foundational sources collected here.

The distinction matters:

- **This page** = the gene's foundational canon. The stable, institutional references that define the established science.
- **Inline page citations** = the specific evidence trail for individual claims. These include newer research, condition-specific literature, and sources for content where PRRT2.org expands beyond the consensus — including papers that post-date or are not yet reflected in the foundational sources below.

Neither replaces the other. This page is where you go for the authoritative baseline. Individual page citations are where you go to trace a specific claim.

For how this site distinguishes established science from its own perspective, see [How to Read This Site](https://www.prrt2.org/how-to-read).

## Core Gene References

### MedlinePlus Genetics — PRRT2 gene
**Audience:** Patients, families, general readers
**Publisher:** U.S. National Library of Medicine, NIH

The plain-language overview of the PRRT2 gene — what it does, what conditions it is associated with, and how it is inherited. The best starting point for anyone new to PRRT2.

[https://medlineplus.gov/genetics/gene/prrt2/](https://medlineplus.gov/genetics/gene/prrt2/)

---

### GeneReviews — PRRT2-Related Disorder
**Audience:** Clinicians, researchers, informed patients
**Publisher:** University of Washington, Seattle — hosted by NCBI / NIH
**Authors:** Yang K, Quiroz V, Ebrahimi-Fakhari D — last updated July 2024

The in-depth clinical reference for the full PRRT2 spectrum — self-limited (familial) infantile epilepsy, paroxysmal kinesigenic dyskinesia, PKD with infantile convulsions, and hemiplegic migraine. Covers diagnosis, management, surveillance, and genetic counseling in detail.

Note: an earlier GeneReviews chapter titled *Familial Paroxysmal Kinesigenic Dyskinesia* (NBK1460) has been **retired** by NCBI and is flagged as potentially out of date. This chapter supersedes it, and is the one to cite.

[https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK475803/](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK475803/)

---

### OMIM — Entry 614386 (PRRT2)
**Audience:** Clinicians, researchers
**Publisher:** Johns Hopkins University

The technical gene and disorder catalog entry for PRRT2. Dense and reference-heavy — written for clinicians and researchers rather than general readers, but the most comprehensive catalog of the genetic and clinical literature.

[https://omim.org/entry/614386](https://omim.org/entry/614386)

---

### NCBI Genetic Testing Registry (GTR)
**Audience:** Patients, clinicians looking for testing options
**Publisher:** National Center for Biotechnology Information, NIH

A searchable registry of genetic tests available for PRRT2-related conditions, including the labs that offer them. Useful when researching testing options or preparing for a conversation with a geneticist.

[https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gtr/conditions/C3150878/](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gtr/conditions/C3150878/)

---

### NORD — PRRT2-Associated Paroxysmal Movement Disorders
**Audience:** Patients, families, patient advocates
**Publisher:** National Organization for Rare Disorders

The rare disease summary for PRRT2-associated conditions from the leading U.S. rare disease patient advocacy organization. Covers symptoms, causes, affected populations, related disorders, and available resources.

[https://rarediseases.org/rare-diseases/prrt2-associated-paroxysmal-movement-disorders/](https://rarediseases.org/rare-diseases/prrt2-associated-paroxysmal-movement-disorders/)
