# Official Resources

This page gathers the authoritative, primary sources on the PRRT2 gene in one place.

Throughout PRRT2.org, we explain this material in plain language and expand on it — but if you want to go straight to the original references, or share them with a healthcare provider, they are collected here. For how this site distinguishes established science from its own perspective, see [How to Read This Site](../how-to-read.md).

## Plain-language overviews

**MedlinePlus Genetics — PRRT2 gene**\
A patient-friendly overview of the PRRT2 gene from the U.S. National Library of Medicine, part of the NIH. The best starting point for a general reader.\
[medlineplus.gov/genetics/gene/prrt2](https://medlineplus.gov/genetics/gene/prrt2/)

**NORD — PRRT2-Associated Paroxysmal Movement Disorders**\
A patient-oriented summary from the National Organization for Rare Disorders.\
[rarediseases.org](https://rarediseases.org/rare-diseases/prrt2-associated-paroxysmal-movement-disorders/)

## In-depth clinical references

**GeneReviews — Familial Paroxysmal Kinesigenic Dyskinesia**\
An expert-authored, peer-reviewed clinical reference, produced by the University of Washington and hosted on the NIH's NCBI Bookshelf. Written for clinicians; the deepest consensus reference.\
[ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK1460](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK1460/)

**OMIM — Entry 614386 (PRRT2)**\
The authoritative catalog entry for the PRRT2 gene and its associated disorders, maintained by Johns Hopkins University. Technical; written for clinicians and researchers.\
[omim.org/entry/614386](https://omim.org/entry/614386)

## Gene and testing databases

**NCBI Gene — PRRT2 (Gene ID 112476)**\
The reference database entry for the gene itself.\
[ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/112476](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/112476)

**NIH Genetic Testing Registry (GTR)**\
Information on genetic tests for PRRT2, and a hub linking to other authoritative resources.\
[ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gtr/genes/112476](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gtr/genes/112476/)

**ClinicalTrials.gov**\
